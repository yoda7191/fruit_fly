Result

Choose two odours on the left and run an experiment. The fly smells the CS+ together with a shock (or sugar reward), and the CS− alone. Afterwards both odours are tested again: if the fly learned, its output neurons answer the CS+ less.

The 3D view replays every spike: neurons flash white when they fire. Watch the sparse Kenyon cell pattern for each odour, and the dopamine neurons during the US. Click any neuron to see what it connects to.
